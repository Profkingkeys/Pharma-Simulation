# Teaching and review notes

Version 0.2 uses authored, deterministic scenarios. Complete the campaign yourself before inviting learners. Ask them to explain why a rejected option is wrong; points are a navigation aid, not a competence score. Exported attempt reports have no names or patient identifiers. Do not add identifying information to a shared report.

Review content, accessibility and transfer of learning separately. A correct game choice does not demonstrate safe professional practice. Record reviewer name, qualification, jurisdiction, date, scenario version, proposed changes and approval scope before formal use. This repository has not yet received that review.

## Pharmacy

These are fictional nonsterile dispensing workflows. They omit quantitative formulae and assume that a reviewed prescription, compatible ingredients and locally applicable professional standards exist. The suspension, semisolid and solution cards illustrate different methods; they are not interchangeable universal recipes. An unknown ingredient is a hazard to quarantine, not an actual poison identified by the software.

Quality includes identity, work-area preparation, method, inspection, suitable packaging, verified label/BUD, documentation, independent release and counseling. The outcome animation is symbolic. It does not model absorption, dose response, disease severity, poisoning or treatment efficacy.

The [FDA compounding Q&A](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/compounding-and-fda-questions-and-answers) explains US compounding risks including contamination and incorrect strength. It does not establish Nigerian or other local standards. Faculty must provide the locally approved curriculum and formulary.
